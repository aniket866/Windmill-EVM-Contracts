## Responsibilities

- Review and merge pull requests for this repository.
- Moderate discussions in the `#windmill-exchange` Discord channel.
- Own the deployment records in `Deployments.md`.
- Keep CI workflows (CI, Slither, gas, fuzz, release) healthy.

## How to reach maintainers

All project communication happens on Discord:

- Join the [Stability Nexus Discord server](https://discord.gg/YzDKeEfWtS).
- Post in the `#windmill-exchange` channel and tag a maintainer.

If a PR is unattended for 1-2 weeks, tag a maintainer on Discord.
